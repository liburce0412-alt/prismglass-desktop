Art direction: clear sculptural liquid optical glass floating over a rich blue-hour dream landscape. Closely tied to a user's luminous blue Windows desktop aesthetic, richer and more tactile than pale white cards. Refined Apple-like restraint and precision, soft cyan, deep cornflower/periwinkle blue, cool moon-silver, a tiny warm peach horizon. A low silky landscape of layered cloud-like hills, a curved horizon and a softly luminous moon/orb create real spatial depth. Calm, inhabited, beautiful atmosphere first. Dark blue upper area provides sharp white text contrast; lower area is luminous blue and lavender. No flat plain white dashboard.

Composition with generous breathing room, usable touch sizes and a purposeful hierarchy:
- Small thin PrismGlass diamond mark and wordmark top left. A very quiet top right connected-computer status with a small green dot, theme icon, settings icon.
- Upper/mid left: small date "10月26日 星期一"; elegant large extremely clean thin white clock "16:28". Below, one quiet line "把此刻，留给喜欢的事。" and a tiny tasteful weather glyph with "23° 多云". These sit directly on the scene, NOT in cards.
- Mid/right: one floating transparent sculpted glass music surface, approximately 30% screen width, with a modest beautiful abstract blue/peach album square, short Chinese title "缓慢晴朗", subtitle "正在播放", a subtle progress hairline and three precise playback symbols. It is substantially CLEAR, the landscape flows through and is visibly magnified and warped toward the perimeter. Give the panel optical thickness, a convex polished bevel, sharply localized white glints and a deep but soft contact shadow. Absolutely no white or milk-filled rectangle.
- Lower left, just above the dock: a small elegant focus capsule showing "25:00" with "留一段专注", and a tiny round luminous glass orb as a decorative breathing/sensory widget. Avoid turning this into a grid.
- Bottom edge: the strongest material focal point, a wide low floating liquid-glass Touch Bar, with visibly convex lens-like edges and scene refraction. It contains six spacious minimal line-icon controls labelled exactly "此刻" "触控板" "工作" "应用" "小组件" "电脑". The active "此刻" is indicated by a clear raised glass lens nested into the bar with restrained optical shine, not a solid white fill. The bar has a physical sense of weight, bottom-edge highlight and caustic, preserving legible unwarped labels. No overlap with content.

Glass truth and craft: show obvious geometric displacement of the SAME cloud horizon behind each glass surface, thick rounded refraction at the sides, broad clear interiors, directional Fresnel reflection, tiny scene-dependent chromatic dispersion only in localized highlights. The scene must remain visible through the glass. No neon border, no permanent rainbow outline, no generic frosted white cards. Only 2–3 priority glass surfaces, not everything in boxes. Text and icons remain razor sharp on top of the material. Use a cohesive neutral sans-serif Chinese UI typography, disciplined spacing, generous 44px+ touch affordances scaled to composition. Realistic implementable UI, no excessive tiny labels, no fabricated long data tables. This must feel like a personal glass instrument and atmospheric desktop extension, not a dashboard template. No watermark.
